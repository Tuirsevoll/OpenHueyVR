HGVR – Bisque Doll and Weeping Stone grips

Put these files into the PROPS folder of your item models mod, the same one that
already has grips.txt and the other item-*.gltf files:

    <your item mod>\library\data\PROPS

On your PC that is usually one of:

    F:\Haunting Ground VR Cursor\OpenHuey-master\openhuey-src\examples\mods\item-props\library\data\PROPS
    F:\Haunting Ground VR Cursor\OpenHuey-master\build\openhuey-src\Release\mods\item-props\library\data\PROPS

If your .bat copies the item mod into the build folder, copy into the source
folder the .bat copies from. Otherwise the next build overwrites it.

What to copy (replace when Windows asks):

    grips.txt
    grip_poses.txt
        Replace the copies already in PROPS. These are the full files, with
        the doll and stone added. Don't use only the new lines.

    item-147.gltf
    item_147_bisque_doll.gltf
    item_147_bisque_doll.bin
        The Bisque Doll (item 147). Keep all three together.

    item-155.gltf
    item_155_weeping_stone.gltf
    item_155_weeping_stone.bin
        The Weeping Stone (item 155). Keep all three together.

    2-1.png
        The shared picture for both models. It must sit in the same PROPS
        folder as the .gltf files (not in a subfolder).

Your other item models are unchanged. Right hand only: the game mirrors the
left hand by itself.

Poses: grip_bisquedoll (item 147) and grip_weepingstone (item 155). The stone
pose includes knuckle slides.
